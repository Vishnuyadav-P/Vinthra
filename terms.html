Vinthra provides technical career development infrastructure, including:

  • Capstone & Final-Year Project Guidance: Domain scoping (AI, ML, Data Science, Full-Stack, DevOps), architecture sign-off, Git repo guidance, and viva presentation rehearsal.

  • Verifiable Proof of Work: Cryptographically shareable digital completion credentials and viva-ready repository portfolios.

4. Milestone-Based Cancellation & Refund Policy

To balance student flexibility with the direct booking of dedicated industry engineers, refunds are governed by the following milestone schedule:

  • Phase 1 — Prior to Mentor Allocation (100% Refund): If a written cancellation request is received within 48 hours of payment and before a dedicated mentor has scoped the architecture, a full refund (less actual third-party payment gateway transaction processing fees, typically 2–3%) will be issued.
  • Phase 2 — Post-Allocation / Architecture Stage (50% Refund): If cancellation is requested after mentor pairing and architecture scoping, but prior to the commencement of Sprint 2 check-ins, a 50% pro-rated refund will be granted.
  • Phase 3 — Active Sprints & Code Review (Non-Refundable): Once active weekly development sprints, code reviews, and viva documentation preparation have commenced, fees are strictly non-refundable due to the irreversible commitment of instructional resources and mentor time.
  • Dispute Notice: Prior to lodging a payment dispute or chargeback with your issuing bank, you agree to notify us in writing at contact@vinthra.com to facilitate an amicable resolution.

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment source within 7–10 Indian banking business days.
